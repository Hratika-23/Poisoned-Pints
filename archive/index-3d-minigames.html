📜 How to Play

🌞Day: scheme. Hire shady staff, upgrade your ale, send agents on secret missions and slip the Guard Captain a bribe.
🌙Night: chaos. Your missions play out. Play them yourself (sneak past guards, win a brawl, pick a pocket) or let your agent try.
🕶️Everything is secret. Rivals never see your crew, plans or bribes, and you never see theirs.
🔍Attacks are anonymous. Collect clues (colours, outfits, spy reports) and accuse the culprit. Right = they're fined. Wrong = you look foolish.
🐀Watch out for traitors. A recruit might secretly work for a rival. Read their notes, and send a Spy to check your own crew.
🏆After 10 nights, the highest gold + reputation × 5 wins.
