📜 How to Play

🌞Day: scheme. Hire shady staff, send them on secret missions, and slip the Guard Captain a bribe.
🌙Night: chaos. Every tavern's agents move at once. In the morning you learn what happened to you.
🕶️Everything is secret. Rivals never see your crew, plans or bribes, and you never see theirs.
🔍Attacks are anonymous, but every crew has known habits (boots, scent, which hand). Clues never lie. Cross off suspects, then accuse.
😄Your crew have moods and quirks. Keep them busy and paid, or they grumble, refuse orders… or sell you out.
🐾Falling behind? Underdogs get cheaper hires, local support, and safer revenge on the leader.
🏆Highest gold + reputation × 5 after the last night wins.
